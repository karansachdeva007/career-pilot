# Adobe Interview Preparation Guide

## Overview
Adobe is known for its strong emphasis on CS fundamentals, specifically OS, DBMS, and Object-Oriented Programming (OOP), in addition to Data Structures and Algorithms.

## Key Focus Areas
- **CS Fundamentals**: Be thoroughly prepared for Operating Systems (Process synchronization, deadlocks, paging), DBMS (SQL queries, normalization, transactions), and Computer Networks.
- **Algorithms**: Moderate to hard algorithmic questions focusing on Trees, Arrays, and Dynamic Programming.
- **OOP Concepts**: Deep understanding of Polymorphism, Inheritance, Virtual Functions (especially if interviewing in C++).

## Interview Process
- **Aptitude & Coding Round**: Includes multiple-choice questions on CS fundamentals and 2-3 coding problems.
- **Technical Rounds (2-3)**: Heavy focus on DS/Algo and core subjects. You might be asked to design small systems or write complex SQL queries.
- **Managerial/HR Round**: Focuses on cultural fit, past projects, and behavioral questions.

## Tips
- Do not neglect your core CS subjects; Adobe asks detailed theoretical questions.
- If you know C++, brush up on pointers and memory management.
