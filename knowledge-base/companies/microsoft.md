# Microsoft Interview Preparation Guide

## Overview
Microsoft interviews generally focus on Data Structures and Algorithms, System Design, and behavioral questions. They test your core CS fundamentals thoroughly and place high value on clean, production-ready code.

## Key Focus Areas
- **Data Structures**: Linked Lists, Trees, Arrays, and Strings.
- **Algorithms**: Sorting, Searching, and Dynamic Programming (moderate difficulty).
- **System Design**: Highly emphasized for senior roles, but even entry-level candidates might face Object-Oriented Design questions.

## Interview Process
- **Screening**: Often an online assessment (OA) or a phone screen with a coding problem.
- **Onsite (Virtual)**: Usually 4-5 rounds.
  - 2-3 Technical Coding Rounds
  - 1 System Design or OOD Round
  - 1 "As Appropriate" (AA) round with a senior leader or hiring manager (Behavioral + Technical).

## Tips
- Always test your code with edge cases.
- Explain your thought process out loud.
- Be ready to discuss trade-offs in your design and algorithms.
