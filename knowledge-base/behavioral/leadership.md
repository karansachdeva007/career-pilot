# Leadership Principles & The STAR Method

## Overview
Many top tech companies (especially Amazon) use Leadership Principles to evaluate candidates. You should prepare stories from your past experience that demonstrate these principles in action.

## The STAR Method
The only acceptable way to answer behavioral questions is using the STAR method:
- **Situation**: Set the scene and provide necessary context.
- **Task**: Describe the challenge or responsibility you faced.
- **Action**: Explain exactly what *you* did (use "I", not "We").
- **Result**: Share the outcome. Quantify the impact if possible.

## Key Leadership Themes

### 1. Handling Ambiguity
*Tell me about a time you had to work on a project with unclear requirements.*
- Show how you took charge, communicated with stakeholders, gathered data, and defined a clear path forward.

### 2. Failing and Learning
*Tell me about a time you failed.*
- Do not blame others. Own the mistake, explain the root cause, and most importantly, detail what you learned and how you changed your process to ensure it never happened again.

### 3. Disagreeing and Committing
*Tell me about a time you disagreed with a manager or team member.*
- Show that you can push back respectfully with data-backed arguments, but once a decision is made, you fully support and execute the chosen path.

### 4. Taking Ownership
*Tell me about a time you went above and beyond your job description.*
- Highlight a time you noticed a broken process, a bug, or an inefficiency, and proactively fixed it without being asked.
