# Common HR / Behavioral Questions

## Overview
HR and behavioral rounds are designed to assess your cultural fit, communication skills, and how you handle conflict and failure.

## Top Questions and How to Answer Them

### 1. "Tell me about yourself."
Keep it to 2 minutes. Use the **Present-Past-Future** formula.
- **Present**: What is your current role and what do you do?
- **Past**: What experiences brought you here? Briefly mention key achievements.
- **Future**: What are you looking for next, and why are you excited about *this* role?

### 2. "Why do you want to work here?"
Show that you've done your research. Talk about the company's product, their mission, or their recent engineering challenges. Don't just say "because it's a good company with good pay."

### 3. "What is your biggest weakness?"
Avoid cliché answers like "I work too hard" or "I'm a perfectionist." Pick a real, professional weakness that isn't a red flag for the role, and immediately explain the steps you are taking to improve it.
*Example*: "I sometimes struggle with delegating tasks because I like being hands-on. However, I've started using project management tools to better assign tickets to my team, which has improved our overall velocity."

### 4. "Tell me about a time you had a conflict with a coworker."
Use the STAR method. Focus on the *Action* you took to resolve the conflict professionally. Show empathy, active listening, and a focus on the shared goal rather than who was right.
