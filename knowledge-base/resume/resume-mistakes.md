# Common Resume Mistakes to Avoid

## 1. Typos and Grammatical Errors
This is the fastest way to get your resume discarded. It shows a lack of attention to detail. Always use spell check, Grammarly, and have a friend review it.

## 2. Rating Your Skills
Avoid adding progress bars or ratings (e.g., "Python: 5/5", "JavaScript: 3/5"). These are subjective and meaningless. What is a 5/5? Can you write a compiler in Python? Just list the skills you are comfortable working with professionally.

## 3. Too Much Jargon
While keywords are important for the ATS, don't stuff your resume so full of acronyms that it becomes unreadable to a human. The recruiter (who is often non-technical) needs to understand what you actually accomplished.

## 4. Irrelevant Experience
If you are applying for a Senior Backend Engineer role, you don't need to list your high school summer job as a lifeguard. Keep the experience relevant to the role you are applying for.

## 5. Unprofessional Email Address
Use a professional email address, ideally `firstname.lastname@gmail.com`. Avoid using university emails if you graduated years ago, as you might lose access to them. And definitely avoid aliases like `coolcoder99@yahoo.com`.
