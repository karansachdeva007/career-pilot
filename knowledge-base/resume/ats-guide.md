# ATS Resume Guide

## What is an ATS?
Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) are software applications used by employers to manage the recruitment process. They parse resumes, extract keywords, and rank candidates based on how well their resume matches the job description.

## How to Beat the ATS
1. **Use Standard Formatting**: Avoid complex layouts, columns, tables, headers, and footers. The ATS might fail to parse them. Stick to simple, single-column layouts.
2. **Standard Headings**: Use predictable headings like "Experience", "Education", "Skills", and "Projects". Don't use creative titles like "My Journey" or "What I Do".
3. **Keywords are King**: Tailor your resume to the specific job description. If the JD asks for "Object-Oriented Programming," use that exact phrase instead of just "OOP".
4. **Chronological Order**: List your experience in reverse-chronological order.
5. **Save as PDF or DOCX**: Unless specified otherwise, always save your resume as a standard PDF. It preserves formatting and is widely supported by modern ATS.

## What NOT to do
- No images, graphics, or logos.
- Do not use text boxes.
- Avoid unusual fonts. Stick to Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman, or Garamond.
